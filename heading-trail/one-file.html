Overview

    Atlas 2.0 ships on November 17 to every team on the Pro plan. This plan covers the price, the rollout and what could go wrong.

    Mira owns the launch. Questions go to the launch channel, not to her inbox.

    Pricing

    Pro stays at $24 per seat each month. Teams of 10 or more seats can switch to the yearly price of $20 per seat.

    Nobody pays more on launch day. Existing plans keep their price until their next renewal.

    Rollout

    Week one is our own teams in Austin and London. Week two adds 10% of Pro workspaces, then everyone in week three.

    Each step waits for two quiet days on the error dashboard before the next one starts.

    Risks

    Imports may run slowly for workspaces with more than 50,000 files. Sam is adding a progress screen for those.

    If sign-in errors double, we pause the rollout and tell admins within an hour.

    Open questions

    Do we email every admin before launch, or show a banner in the app first? Priya will decide by Friday.
